# Steeplify — Waitlist

Landing page and waitlist for **Steeplify**, the AI-powered business operating system:
everything your business needs to start, operate, and grow, simplified in one place.

- `index.html` — the whole page (styles, scripts, logo and favicon are embedded)
- `logo.png`, `favicon.png` — brand assets

Signups (first name, last name, email) are sent to Formspree; set `WAITLIST_ENDPOINT`
in `index.html` to change where they go.
